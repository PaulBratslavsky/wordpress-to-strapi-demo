The challenge

Summit Trail's online store was slow on phones, and most of their customers shop from a trailhead with one bar of signal. Checkout took six steps.

{{image:backpack|Product photography for the new store.}}

Our approach

  1. Rebuilt the product pages to load in under two seconds on 3G
  2. Cut checkout to two steps, with saved addresses
  3. Wrote trail guides that link to the gear they mention
{{image:red-rocks|One of the trail guides: a weekend loop through red rock country.}}

Results

Mobile conversion rate doubled, and the trail guides now bring in a third of all organic traffic.
