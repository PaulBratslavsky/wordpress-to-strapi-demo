The challenge

Atlas Books sells rare and second-hand books. Every title is one of a kind, so the catalogue changes daily and product descriptions matter more than photos.

{{image:antique-books|A few recent arrivals.}}

Our approach

  • A listing form that takes staff under two minutes per book
  • Search by author, edition and condition
  • A "just arrived" email sent automatically every Friday

Results

Online sales now make up half of the shop's revenue, up from a tenth.
