The challenge

Voltline installs rooftop solar for homeowners. Their customers wanted to know one thing: is it saving me money? The existing app showed kilowatt-hours and not much else.

{{image:solar-roof|A typical Voltline installation.}}

Our approach

We designed a dashboard around money saved rather than energy produced, and built it on a headless CMS so the same content powers the app, the website and the monthly email.
