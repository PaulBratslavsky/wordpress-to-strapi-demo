The challenge

Maple Street had been baking in the same building since 1962. The new owners wanted to modernise without losing the regulars who'd been coming in for decades.

{{gallery:bread-shelves,bakery-display|The morning bake.}}

Our approach

We restored the original 1960s shop sign lettering and used it across a new menu board, packaging and a simple website with today's specials, updated by the staff each morning.

Results

Pre-orders through the website now sell out the weekend sourdough by Thursday.
