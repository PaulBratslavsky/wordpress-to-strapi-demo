The challenge

Patients couldn't find opening hours, and the phone line was busy all morning with questions the website should have answered.

{{image:clinic-room|The clinic's new treatment rooms, photographed for the redesigned site.}}

Our approach

We interviewed front-desk staff, listed the twenty most common calls, and made sure each one had a clear answer within two clicks of the home page. Every page was tested with a screen reader.

Results

  • Morning phone calls down 35%
  • Online bookings up from 10% to 45% of appointments
