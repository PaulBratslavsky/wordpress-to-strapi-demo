The challenge

Riverbend had outgrown its hand-drawn logo. With a second café opening and wholesale customers asking for branded bags, the owners needed an identity that worked on everything from a paper cup to a delivery van.

Our approach

We kept the warmth of the original sketch and built a system around it: a hand-lettered wordmark, a palette taken from the roasting process, and packaging that shows the origin of each bean.

Results

  • Wholesale orders up 40% in the first six months
  • New café opened on schedule with signage, menus and cups ready

People recognise our bags across the room now. That never happened before.
