After six years of working from kitchen tables and borrowed meeting rooms, Northfield Studio finally has a home of its own. We signed the lease in June and spent the summer painting, building desks and arguing about where the coffee machine should go. [caption id="attachment_{{id:studio-desk}}" align="aligncenter" width="1024"] Jordan's desk on moving day. It has not been this tidy since.[/caption] The space used to be a print shop, which is why there are still type drawers in the hallway. We kept them. Some things you just don't throw away.

What's in the new studio

  • Six desks, with room for two more
  • A proper meeting room (with a door!)
  • A workshop wall for sticky notes
