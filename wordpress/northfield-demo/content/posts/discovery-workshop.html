Every project starts with a half-day discovery workshop. Clients often ask what actually happens in the room, so here's an honest walkthrough.

The first hour: goals

Everyone writes down what success looks like a year from now, one idea per sticky note. Then we group the notes and vote. The top three become the project's goals.

Before and after the workshop

Before

  • "We need a new website."
  • Twelve stakeholders, twelve priorities
  • No agreed budget or deadline

After

  • Three measurable goals
  • One decision-maker
  • A phased plan with a first release date

What to bring

  • Access to your analytics
  • Three websites you admire, and one you don't
  • The person who signs off on the project
