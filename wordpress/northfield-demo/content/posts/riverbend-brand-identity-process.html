Riverbend Coffee Roasters came to us with great coffee and a logo drawn on a napkin in 2011. They were opening a second café and starting wholesale, and the napkin wasn't going to survive being printed on a 5 kg bag. Here's how the new identity came together.

{{image:coffee-roaster|The roaster at Riverbend's first café, where most of our research happened.}}

Start with the product, not the logo

We spent two mornings behind the counter before sketching anything. The things regulars loved most were the roasting smell, the handwritten menu and the owners knowing everyone's order. None of that was in the old brand.

{{gallery:coffee-beans,coffee-shop,pantone|Research wall: the beans, the café and the colour references we kept coming back to.}}

Our process in five steps

  1. Interview the owners and five regular customers.
  2. Collect everything the brand already touches: cups, bags, signs, the website.
  3. Explore three directions in black and white.
  4. Pick one and add colour and type.
  5. Test it on real packaging before calling it done.
